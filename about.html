About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star started with weekend bread batches for neighbors. The bakery grew into a place to pick up breakfast, bring home a loaf, or plan a celebration.

How we source ingredients

We look for flour from regional mills and use seasonal fruit when it is available from nearby growers.

Meet the bakery team

Mia shapes and bakes the morning bread. Alex leads pastry prep and helps customers plan celebration orders.
